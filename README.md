
# Excel Sales & Finance Analytics

## 📊 Sales Report

### Project Objectives
- Developed a [Customer Performance Report](https://github.com/abhishek0x1x/Excel-Sales-Analytics/blob/main/CustomerSalesPerformanceReport.pdf) to analyze customer-wise sales performance and identify key sales trends.
- Created a [Market Performance vs. Sales Targets Report](https://github.com/abhishek0x1x/Excel-Sales-Analytics/blob/main/MarketPerformanceVsTargetSalesReport.pdf) to compare actual sales with targets and identify performance gaps across different markets.

### Business Insights & Purpose
- Analyzed customer purchasing patterns and sales performance.
- Identified sales trends and tracked key performance indicators (KPIs).
- Evaluated market performance against sales targets.
- Explored opportunities for improving customer discounts and sales strategies.
- Identified potential growth opportunities across different markets.

## 💰 Finance Report

### Project Objectives
Created Profit & Loss (P&L) reports to evaluate financial performance across different periods and markets.

1. [P&L Report by Fiscal Year](https://github.com/abhishek0x1x/Excel-Sales-Analytics/blob/main/P%26LbyFiscalYear.pdf)

### Business Insights & Purpose
- Evaluated financial performance across different fiscal years and months.
- Analyzed profitability across different markets.
- Compared financial performance across different time periods.
- Identified trends and variations in financial performance.
- Understood how financial reports support budgeting, forecasting, and business decision-making.
